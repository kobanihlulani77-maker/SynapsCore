## 8. Follow-up message structure

Every first follow-up should be shorter than the first email.

Required structure:
1. one sentence identifying the original note;
2. one sentence restating SynapseCore as the whole connected operational intelligence/control system;
3. one sentence explaining why that company was selected;
4. direct routing question to the appropriate operational owner;
5. 20–30 minute discovery request.

Do not repeat:
- long product explanation;
- detailed workflow examples;
- integration architecture;
- pricing;
- pilot proposal;
- claims about company pain.

---

## 9. Follow-up outcome classification

After follow-up, classify each account as:
- **Routed** — someone points us to the right person;
- **Engaged** — operational discussion requested/accepted;
- **Need information** — asks for more material;
- **Existing capability objection** — requires control-tower/whole-loop discovery;
- **No fit / no interest** — close respectfully;
- **No response / route unresolved** — not market rejection;
- **Delivery failure** — fix route;
- **Procurement route** — separate commercial process.

---

## 10. Operational rule

Do not measure outreach success by the number of emails sent.

Measure progress by evidence movement:

**researched company -> correct operational owner -> company-confirmed operating model -> confirmed incremental control need (if any) -> source/data feasibility -> qualified pilot thesis**.

The next first-wave action is a fresh inbox check after the third full business day, then selective follow-up only where justified.
