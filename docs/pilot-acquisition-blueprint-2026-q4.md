## Objective and two parallel workstreams

Prepare one technically defensible controlled real-company pilot while building a second qualified opportunity. The original stretch aspiration is two companies, **staged**, not simultaneous unverified live launches. No year-end date overrides engineering or customer approval. The intended observation period is approximately 40 days per company, to be reconciled with the official six-week pilot program and agreed with each customer.

- **Engineering:** M1 hardening -> M2 repeatable hosted verification -> M3 final operator UI/UX -> M4 adversarial examination -> M5 remediation and independent re-verification -> M6 **mandatory final whole connected-engine acceptance and owner technical sign-off** -> M7 company-specific integration, always-on environment, scoped connected-engine re-verification and joint launch approval -> M8 supervised real-company pilot.
- **Commercial:** qualified prospect research -> permitted introduction -> discovery -> operational/technical qualification -> controlled demonstration of verified functionality -> written proposed scope and commercial/legal terms -> conditional agreement -> customer-specific intake and launch preparation -> M7 approval -> M8.
- Commercial discovery and non-sensitive preparation may proceed during M1-M6; no customer production provisioning, live traffic or launch is authorized by outreach or conditional agreement.

## Prospect fit

Prefer operationally complex retailers, distributors, warehouse operators, 3PLs and e-commerce fulfillment organizations with fragmented sources, orders, inventory, fulfillment and recovery workflows. A controlled **regional, multi-site or distribution-center-plus-stores** lane is a desirable discovery candidate because it can demonstrate cross-domain value; it is **not** a claim that the current hosted candidate has proven that envelope. A customer's actual pilot footprint must be bounded by supported connector contracts, measured capacity, approved roles, operational ownership, and M6/M7 evidence. Avoid a trivial single-store demo and avoid an unbounded enterprise rollout.

Initial research candidates, not verified opportunities or claims of need: Mr Price Group; TFG/Sportscene; Ackermans/Pepkor; Boxer; and qualifying Gauteng/Pretoria distributors or fulfillment operators. Before adding any prospect to the active list, verify the legal entity, applicable operations, relevant department and appropriate contact channel. Do not invent named personnel, private addresses or operational problems.

## People and qualification

Seek (1) an operational sponsor who owns the business problem and approval path, (2) an operational champion who understands exceptions and daily workflows, and (3) a technical/security contact who controls source-system access and integration feasibility. Where required, include procurement/legal and privacy stakeholders.

Discovery must establish: current systems and authoritative sources; specific fragmented workflow; measurable consequence and baseline; current manual response; approved source path and data sensitivity; multi-site/warehouse requirements; decision authority and support contacts; fallback/rollback; practical agreed value metric; budget/contracting path and availability. A friendly response is not a qualified pilot.

## Outreach material controls

**Package A, during M1:** truthful one-page explanation, 30/60-second positioning, specific candidate pains presented as hypotheses, simple diagram of supported operational flow, short founder introduction and meeting request. Describe the platform as rule-based operational intelligence and control above existing systems, not ERP/WMS replacement or an AI agent. The system of record remains with approved customer sources.

**Package B, after sufficient verified proof:** purpose-built demo of supported workflows with clearly marked synthetic data, role-appropriate UI, known limitations and dated evidence. Never present old passing tests, healthy endpoints, unverified production capacity or historic pilot-ready documents as current release acceptance.

**Package C, for a qualified company:** written scope, permitted source/data mappings, operating envelope and exclusions, success/stop measures, ~40-day observation proposal, pricing/commercial assumptions subject to agreement, support, recovery, data/privacy/security and technical/launch conditions. Protect IP; give technical reviewers appropriate evidence without publicly releasing sensitive implementation or secrets.

Use appropriate business contact/consent channels, honor objections, keep prospect personal data minimal and restrict CRM access. Obtain company authorization and necessary confidentiality/data arrangements before requesting non-public documents, real datasets or credentials; use approved secure credential exchange.

## Readiness, cost and stop rules

Render Free is an engineering evidence environment, not a pilot capacity guarantee. Diagnose application/configuration/test/infrastructure causes before upgrades. Any paid experiment requires proven need, cost comparison and owner approval; an actual pilot requires measured, always-on infrastructure and required hosted proof. Technical acceptance requires the full M6 connected-engine record; M7 requires applicable integrated reruns on the exact customer deployment. Never disable security, dilute tests, increase timeouts to hide failures or offer unsupported execution. Stop if authority, correctness, isolation, recovery, source mapping or accepted performance is unresolved.
