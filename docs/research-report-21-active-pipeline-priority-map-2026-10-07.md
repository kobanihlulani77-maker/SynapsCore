**Date:** 2026-10-07 (Africa/Johannesburg)
**Purpose:** Separate strategic product fit, first-pilot practicality, route quality and discovery value across the 16 active October accounts. No score implies customer interest or qualification.

## Decision dimensions

- **Whole-picture fit:** How strongly the public operating model can exercise SynapseCore's connected truth -> intelligence -> priority -> governance -> outcome -> recovery loop.
- **Pilot geometry:** How realistically a meaningful company slice can be bounded without reducing SynapseCore to a single-domain demo.
- **Route quality:** Whether outreach currently reaches a named operational owner or depends on internal routing.
- **Discovery value:** How much we can learn about the market/product even if the company does not become Customer 1.
- **Complexity burden:** Organisational, regulatory, technical, procurement or distributed-authority burden likely before launch.

These are research judgments, not company-confirmed findings.
