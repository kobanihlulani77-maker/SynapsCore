# SynapseCore — Company Information Brief
**Founder-led South African operational intelligence and control system**

## What SynapseCore is
SynapseCore is an operational intelligence and control system for organisations whose operations span multiple systems, teams, locations and external partners.

It sits above the relevant operational systems already used by the company and builds one connected operational picture for the agreed operating scope. From that picture, SynapseCore helps authorised operators understand what is happening, identify changing pressure and priorities, receive evidence-backed recommendations, make governed decisions, and keep the resulting outcome, audit trail and recovery state visible.

SynapseCore is **not** an ERP, WMS, TMS, BI dashboard, generic integration platform or autonomous AI agent.

## The connected operating loop
**authoritative company facts -> connected operational truth -> cross-domain conditions/pressure -> prioritisation and advisory recommendations -> authorised human decision -> supported action/external handoff -> observed outcome -> audit/realtime readback -> replay/recovery when needed**

The systems that already run the business remain authoritative for the functions they own.

## What a controlled pilot is intended to prove
The pilot is not a single order feed or dashboard demonstration.

It selects a meaningful company operating scope and connects the authoritative records needed across the relevant supported domains so that the **whole connected SynapseCore control engine** can be exercised.

The data footprint can be selective:
- selected region/site network instead of the entire company;
- required operational records rather than full databases;
- required fields rather than every available field;
- agreed current/representative data rather than unnecessary history.

But the SynapseCore role remains whole-picture: operational truth, intelligence, priorities, recommendations, operator authority, outcome visibility, audit and recovery working together.

## Existing enterprise systems remain useful
A company may already use SAP, Oracle, Manhattan, specialist WMS/TMS platforms, automation, control towers, analytics or internally built systems.

SynapseCore does not assume those systems are deficient and does not require replacing them.

The discovery question is whether there is incremental value in a connected operational intelligence/control layer across the agreed scope — and whether the required authoritative facts can be supplied through approved interfaces.

If the company's existing control model already closes that loop adequately, SynapseCore should not manufacture a problem.

## Human authority
Recommendations are advisory. Human operators retain operational authority unless a separately implemented and explicitly approved SynapseCore workflow supports a specific direct action.

Hypothetical scenario approval is not business execution.

## Reliability and trust
SynapseCore is designed around source identity and freshness, role/tenant/warehouse authority, audit and event history, integration health, replay/recovery, realtime notification plus authoritative readback, and explicit distinction between actual and hypothetical state.

## Current stage
SynapseCore is being prepared for controlled South African company pilots.

Commercial discovery can proceed now, but live customer launch remains subject to the engineering acceptance programme and company-specific verification. No outreach or discovery conversation by itself authorises production use.

## Initial discussion
The first conversation is normally 20–30 minutes and is focused on understanding the company's current operating model, existing control capability, authoritative systems, decision ownership and whether a meaningful incremental SynapseCore role exists.

No system access or customer data is required for that first discussion.
