# SynapseCore Research Report 06 — South African Operational-Control Market Architecture
**Date:** 2026-10-03  
**Purpose:** Understand how operational control is already bought and delivered in South Africa so SynapseCore can enter the market deliberately.  
**Important:** This is market architecture research, not a claim of superiority or a feature ranking.

## 1. Core conclusion

South African buyers already purchase operational-control capability in several forms:
1. managed logistics control towers;
2. integrated transport/logistics technology;
3. specialist digital control platforms;
4. internal enterprise control functions;
5. ERP/WMS/TMS/planning + BI + human coordination;
6. custom integration/consulting arrangements.

The strongest local benchmark found so far is **Transnova + Tiger Brands** because it shows a buyer purchasing not only technology but a combined operating model: TMS, data integration, planning, carrier orchestration, exception management, business intelligence, people, processes, governance and a long-term improvement relationship.

SynapseCore should therefore not be sold initially as "just another dashboard" or a self-serve SaaS account. For Customer 1, the strongest commercial model appears to be **software + supervised operational implementation + measured pilot outcomes**, while retaining the longer-term option of a lighter software-led deployment once onboarding and control patterns are repeatable.

That is a strategic recommendation, not a pricing decision.

## 2. Transnova — strongest local benchmark for managed control

### Publicly documented model
Transnova describes a Logistics Control Tower as a centralised service using trained people, technology and decision rules to integrate data from multiple sources, identify risks/opportunities early and proactively manage supply-chain disruptions.

Tiger Brands' control-tower case is especially important:
- centralized Logistics Control Tower established from 2016;
- TMS + BI;
- multi-carrier model with 60+ contracted carriers by 2026;
- planning, urgent-order management, reverse logistics and supply-chain reporting;
- jointly staffed operating model;
- predictive ETA and exception management;
- performance governance;
- reported R300m+ cumulative transport/efficiency savings over the relationship;
- over R900m annual transport disbursements managed;
- 605,000+ loads to 190,000+ locations.

Sources:
- https://transnova.co.za/solutions/logistics-control-tower
- https://transnova.co.za/2026/07/20/transnova-expanded-as-tiger-brands-lead-logistics-partner-after-open-tender/
- https://transnova.co.za/2025/12/05/tiger-brands-and-transnova-win-gold-awardat-the-2025-logistics-achiever-awards/
- https://transnova.co.za/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Case_Study_-_Tiger_Brands_-_Logistics_Control_Tower.pdf

These are Transnova/Tiger-reported outcomes, not independent SynapseCore benchmarks.

### What SynapseCore should learn
- The buyer values **operating outcomes**, not software features alone.
- Control capability can be jointly staffed.
- The control layer can sit above multiple carriers/providers.
- Data integration and standard operating processes are part of the delivery.
- Measurable savings/service outcomes support long-term commercial continuity.
- The relationship can expand after proving value.
- Performance-aligned commercial structures are possible in this market.

### Important overlap
Transnova already addresses integration, single operational view, planning, exception handling, decision support and verified logistics execution context. Do not reduce it to a transport dashboard.

### Important difference to investigate, not assume
SynapseCore's intended problem spans operational truth/intelligence/governance/recovery across broader company operations, not only transport. However, this is a product-scope statement, not evidence that SynapseCore is commercially superior or that Transnova cannot expand upstream/downstream.

## 3. DSV — global control-tower / multi-LSP operating service

DSV South Africa describes SCM Services that:
- integrate stakeholders on one collaborative platform;
- use specialised control towers;
- oversee flows from order receipt to shipment delivery;
- manage exception escalations;
- provide SKU/PO/shipment/container-level status;
- use realtime/predictive insights;
- offer PO management and multi-LSP coordination.

Sources:
- https://www.dsv.com/en-za/digital-solutions/supply-chain-management
- https://www.dsv.com/en-za/insights/expert-opinions/control-towers

### What SynapseCore should learn
- A buyer may outsource operational coordination to a global service organisation.
- Multi-provider control is a mature offering.
- Local market buyers will compare SynapseCore not only to software licences, but to managed services with global operations expertise.
- Integration and exception handling are baseline expectations in serious control propositions.

### Market role
Direct/adjacent competitor in logistics-control outcomes, benchmark, and potentially an ecosystem provider inside a customer's broader SynapseCore operating environment.

## 4. Unitrans — control embedded inside a logistics operator

Unitrans describes its control tower as centralised operational control supported by multiple technologies. Public materials describe:
- fleet/transport data centralisation;
- operational and safety exception monitoring;
- performance metrics;
- route optimisation;
- integrated operational systems;
- realtime tracking;
- KPI-driven customer visibility;
- tailored end-to-end logistics solutions.

Sources:
- https://www.unitransafrica.com/the-unitrans-control-tower/
- https://www.unitransafrica.com/about-us/technology/
- https://www.unitransafrica.com/solutions/logistics-and-distribution/

### What SynapseCore should learn
- Operational-control capability can be built into the provider that performs the physical work.
- Safety and compliance authority can be inseparable from the operational platform in some sectors.
- Customer KPIs can be embedded into the service model.
- A first SynapseCore customer that already outsources operations to Unitrans may have less direct control-layer opportunity in that logistics slice, but may still have broader enterprise coordination needs.

## 5. Vector Logistics — cold-chain operator with integrated service control

Vector Logistics publicly reports:
- 27 distribution sites;
- 440 vehicles;
- 5,600 drop points;
- 311,000 cases delivered daily;
- one integrated Service Centre Control Tower;
- multi-temperature cold-chain operations across Southern Africa.

Source:
- https://www.vectorlog.com/

### What SynapseCore should learn
- High-volume physical operations can justify centralized control.
- Cold-chain environments add safety, quality and specialist authority that a general operational layer must respect.
- Where Vector is the customer's logistics provider, its control tower may already own much of the transport/distribution control loop.

## 7. South African buyer expectations we should assume until disproved

For a serious operational-control sale, expect buyers to ask about:
- source integrations and data ownership;
- operational freshness and trust;
- exceptions and prioritisation;
- who operates the system;
- authority and access control;
- implementation effort;
- change management;
- measurable service/cost outcomes;
- support and recovery;
- security;
- scalability;
- how existing ERP/WMS/TMS/3PL investments remain useful.

SynapseCore's pilot packaging must answer these directly.

## 8. Recommended Customer-1 commercial delivery model

### Not recommended initially
**Pure self-service SaaS:** customer signs up, connects systems, figures out operating rules and value alone.

Reason: the product is entering a complex operational environment where data semantics, roles, scope, decision authority and value baselines need deliberate setup.

### Recommended
**Supervised operational pilot**
- SynapseCore software;
- founder-led problem/scoping discovery;
- controlled integration/onboarding;
- explicit operator/authority setup;
- operator training;
- measured baseline;
- monitored operating period;
- regular review of recommendation usefulness, exceptions and trust;
- technical/recovery support;
- measured final value review.

This is consistent with the M7/M8 program and lets the company experience SynapseCore as an operating capability, not merely a screen.

### Longer-term evolution
After repeated deployments establish reusable integration, configuration, operator and value patterns, parts of onboarding/support can become standardized or self-service.

## 9. Strategic implication for sales language

Do not lead with:
- "one dashboard";
- "replace your ERP";
- "we connect everything";
- "AI control tower";
- "you have fragmented systems";
- "your WMS cannot do this".

Lead with:
**How does your operation maintain one trustworthy understanding of what is happening across the systems and teams involved in a customer/business outcome, decide what matters, route the right response to the right authority, and verify that the outcome actually happened?**

Then listen.
