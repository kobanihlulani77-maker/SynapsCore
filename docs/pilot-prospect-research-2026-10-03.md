# Pilot Prospect Research — Operational Dossiers
**Date:** 2026-10-03  
**Status:** Working research, not customer qualification.  
**Purpose:** Identify organizations whose real operating complexity may expose a meaningful SynapseCore control/intelligence problem. Facts, hypotheses and open questions are deliberately separated.

## Research rule
Do not qualify a prospect because it is large, has many branches, or uses many systems. Qualify only when:
1. operational dependencies create meaningful consequences;
2. important prioritisation/coordination/recovery work remains unresolved or human-heavy;
3. SynapseCore can address a meaningful portion of that problem through a supported pilot lane;
4. the problem can be measured and safely bounded.

The full SynapseCore lens is: operational truth -> pressure/risk intelligence -> prioritisation -> advisory recommendations -> governance/authority -> operator control -> escalation -> recovery -> realtime convergence -> audit/outcome.

## 1. BMG / Invicta RPI

### Verified public facts
- Invicta's RPI division includes BMG and reports a network of 117 BMG branches plus 92 vendor-managed-inventory (VMI) consignment sites.
- RPI activities include international/local sourcing, engineering-component distribution, technical support, value-added assembly and customised solutions.
- BMG warehouse roles explicitly require urgent customer requirements to be prioritised, WMS dashboards to be monitored, operational issues escalated and corrective action taken.
- A 2026 BMG Inbound Manager role describes Kerridge ERP and Manhattan SCALE WMS for yard management, ASNs, receiving queues, receiving exceptions, inventory accuracy, dock utilisation and service levels.
- BMG's warehouse environment is therefore already ERP/WMS controlled; the research opportunity is not basic stock or warehouse digitisation.

### Source record
- Invicta FY2026 executive review: https://invictaholdings.co.za/executive-review-of-performance/
- BMG warehouse supervisor vacancy: https://bmgworld.net/medias/Warehouse-Ops-Supervisor-M4469.pdf
- 2026 Inbound Manager evidence: public BMG/Glassdoor job listing indexed in October 2026.

### Abnormal-event hypothesis
A critical industrial customer requirement cannot be fulfilled from the expected stock location. Another branch/VMI site may hold stock, central inventory is constrained, incoming supply is uncertain and multiple customer commitments compete for the same resource.

### Existing systems likely know
- item and stock records;
- receiving and warehouse state;
- order/pick work;
- location/process state;
- inbound queues and exceptions.

### Human/control questions
- How is customer consequence represented beyond order priority?
- How are competing urgent requirements ranked across branches/VMI sites?
- Who combines ERP, WMS, customer/service and supply information?
- How are alternative responses identified and compared?
- Who owns escalation and decision authority?
- How is a decision tracked to verified resolution?
- Can management see unresolved operational pressure across the network, not only local warehouse queues?

### SynapseCore hypothesis
A bounded BMG lane could test whether SynapseCore can correlate accepted operational facts, surface pressure, prioritise exceptions, provide advisory next actions, route attention/authority and maintain one realtime/auditable view of the issue through recovery.

### What would invalidate the opportunity
- BMG already has an enterprise control/intelligence layer covering this exact end-to-end decision loop;
- required source data cannot be made available through a supported pilot integration;
- the useful scope depends on unsupported direct execution or highly specialised industrial integrations;
- no operational sponsor identifies this as a meaningful problem.

### Discovery owner targets
Operations/logistics leadership, inventory/network operations, customer-service/fulfilment operations, and technical/system ownership for ERP/WMS integrations.

## 2. VKB Group / selected operating division

### Verified public facts
- VKB Agriculture reports 58 retail outlets in its Commercial Division and distribution centres at Bethlehem and Mokopane created to improve product availability.
- A May 2026 Demand Coordinator role required alignment across Sales, Procurement, Production and Logistics using forecasts, reporting, Microsoft NAV and BI; deviations above thresholds were to be escalated for corrective action.
- A June 2026 Systems Controller role described Microsoft Navision, stock-movement integrity, FIFO/location controls, pallet history, quarantine, adjustments and stock-take monitoring.
- A June 2026 Multi Green Operations Coordinator role described ownership of orders from receipt to fulfilment, production planning, bottleneck resolution, stock/demand visibility, procurement coordination, logistics communication and acting as the central link between production, sales and logistics.

### Source record
- VKB retail: https://www.vkb.co.za/index.php/en/agriculture/departments/retail
- Demand Coordinator, May 2026: https://www.myjobmag.co.za/job/demand-coordinator-vkb-milling-reitz-vkb-group
- Systems Controller, June 2026: https://www.myjobmag.co.za/job/systems-controller-vkb-milling-reitz-vkb-group-1
- Multi Green Operations Coordinator, June 2026: https://www.myjobmag.co.za/job/operations-coordinator-multi-green-villiers-vkb-group

### Key research signal
VKB publicly shows that people are responsible for combining forecasts, ERP/BI data, production, procurement, logistics and service commitments, detecting deviations, resolving bottlenecks and escalating corrective action. This is direct evidence of a human operational-coordination layer above transactional systems.

### Abnormal-event hypothesis
Demand for a time-sensitive agricultural product rises beyond plan. Raw material or supplier availability is constrained, production capacity is limited, DC/branch stock is uneven, logistics capacity is finite and customer deadlines differ.

### Existing systems/processes likely know
- ERP stock and movements;
- forecasts and sales history;
- production state/plans;
- procurement requirements;
- logistics/delivery state;
- branch availability.

### Human/control questions
- How quickly are cross-functional consequences assembled into one picture?
- What determines which branch/customer/service commitment is most at risk?
- Are threshold deviations prioritised by business consequence or simply escalated?
- How are proposed corrective actions compared?
- Where are decisions, ownership and due times recorded?
- Does everyone receive the resulting state immediately?
- How does the team distinguish stale/bad data from a real operational condition?
- How much coordination still happens through forecast packs, meetings, spreadsheets, email or calls?

### SynapseCore hypothesis
A selected division such as Multi Green, Milling or a controlled retail/DC lane may offer a full-loop test: accepted facts -> demand/stock/fulfilment pressure -> priority -> recommendation -> human decision/escalation -> observed outcome.

### What would invalidate the opportunity
- the selected division already has an integrated control layer providing the same decision/governance/recovery loop;
- integration would require unsupported production-control interfaces before meaningful value can be shown;
- useful scope cannot be isolated from regulated/safety-critical workflows;
- no division-level owner has a measurable coordination pain.

### Discovery owner targets
Demand/supply planning, operations, commercial/retail operations, production/logistics coordination and business systems.

## 3. Masterparts

### Verified public facts
- Masterparts reports more than 60,000 stocked items for over 4,500 vehicle models and more than 600 employees across 16 branches.
- Gauteng includes Hennopspark, Montana, Randburg and a Modderfontein Distribution Centre; Boksburg is opening in 2026.
- Modderfontein is a distribution centre with no retail counter and a call-centre function.
- Account customers can use an online ordering system.
- Current 2026 warehouse roles at Modderfontein and branches involve high-volume receiving, checking, picking, packing, stock counts and freight preparation.
- Branches provide delivery to business customers in their local areas.

### Source record
- Masterparts company profile: https://www.masterparts.com/about-masterparts/
- Branch directory: https://www.masterparts.com/branches/
- Modderfontein DC: https://www.masterparts.com/branches/modderfontein/
- Online system: https://www.masterparts.com/online-shop-2/
- Current Modderfontein warehouse evidence: public October 2026 Masterparts job listing.

### Research signal
The network combines a large SKU universe, branch/DC structure, account ordering, local delivery and technically specific automotive requirements. Public evidence currently shows strong transactional and warehouse execution but does not establish an enterprise-wide exception prioritisation/control layer. Absence of public evidence is not proof that one does not exist.

### Abnormal-event hypothesis
A workshop needs a specific part urgently. The local branch cannot fulfil it; the Gauteng DC has limited quantity; another branch has competing demand; replenishment timing is uncertain; delivery windows differ and the customer's vehicle/job is waiting.

### Human/control questions
- Is urgency captured explicitly or mainly handled by sales/branch judgement?
- How are competing requests against limited stock ranked?
- Can operators see cross-branch alternatives and downstream delivery consequence together?
- What happens when a promised part cannot be fulfilled as expected?
- Who owns the exception from discovery to resolution?
- How are customers/branches updated?
- Can management see unresolved exceptions and systemic pressure?
- Are recovery actions and outcomes auditable?

### SynapseCore hypothesis
A Gauteng pilot could potentially be bounded around Modderfontein DC plus selected branches, using one supported operational data lane to test exception visibility, prioritisation, recommendations, operator ownership and outcome tracking without replacing Masterparts' ordering or warehouse systems.

### What would invalidate the opportunity
- Masterparts already has a mature internal control/exception platform covering these functions;
- their ordering/inventory interfaces cannot support a controlled integration without substantial custom development;
- branch/DC priority decisions are too low-value or infrequent to justify a pilot;
- the required value depends on automated allocation/execution that SynapseCore does not currently support.

### Discovery owner targets
Gauteng distribution/operations management, branch operations leadership, head-office systems/IT and commercial/customer-service operations.

## Phase 2 — existing-control audit (2026-10-03)

**Method:** Public operating/technology evidence is a capability clue, not evidence of an unresolved customer pain. Distinguish selected software from verified live rollout; an existing WMS/ERP/BI function from an end-to-end control loop; and hypothetical benefit from current SynapseCore proof. No named company has confirmed a problem or agreed to a pilot.

### BMG: what is already covered
- November 12, 2025, Supply Chain Junction announced BMG's selection of Manhattan SCALE to modernize warehouse systems and improve efficiency and delivery: https://www.scjunction.com/blog/bmg-selects-manhattan-scale-to-drive-supply-chain-efficiency
- Current FY2026 Invicta reporting identifies operational optimisation, inventory positioning and working-capital management as group concerns: https://invictaholdings.co.za/executive-review-of-performance/
- Official BMG business contact route: BMG World, 38 Droste Crescent, Jeppestown, Johannesburg; main telephone +27 11 620 1500; customercare@bmgworld.net. This is a general business route, not a verified operations sponsor: https://bmgworld.net/branch-locator

**Audit interpretation:** Do not pitch warehouse management, basic stock visibility, order processing or generic control-tower concepts without establishing an uncovered use case. Selection of Manhattan SCALE is verified; current live implementation breadth, dates and enterprise integration are not verified by the announcement alone.

**Decisive discovery test:** Trace a cross-branch/VMI urgent-customer conflict through ERP, WMS, service commitments, decision authority, escalation and resolution. Ask how exceptions are ranked by consequence, who owns the case and whether the final result is verified in authoritative systems. An existing satisfactory cross-network decision platform or no supported source-data path disqualifies the proposed pilot lane.

### VKB / Multi Green: what is already covered
- Multi Green Operations Coordinator posting June 24, 2026 lists order-to-fulfilment ownership, production scheduling/bottlenecks, stock and demand visibility, procurement and real-time communication between production, sales and logistics. Lists Excel and **Business Central**: https://www.myjobmag.co.za/job/operations-coordinator-multi-green-villiers-vkb-group
- Microsoft documents that Business Central includes supply planning, MPS/MRP and planned purchase, transfer or production orders. Availability of the product features does **not** demonstrate whether Multi Green has configured/uses them: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/production-planning
- VKB's official Multi Green page describes a fertiliser mixing and distribution operation serving an agent network: https://www.vkb.co.za/index.php/en/agriculture/companies/multi-green
- Official general contact: VKB head office +27 58 863 8111; official Multi Green number 058 821 1087. These are business channels only, not confirmed pilot contacts: https://www.vkb.co.za/index.php/en/?id=19&view=category

**Audit interpretation:** Do not pitch production planning or forecasting features that Business Central may already provide. Investigate the distinct coordination layer: which cross-functional exceptions remain difficult to explain, prioritize, assign, govern, escalate and verify across existing systems and operators.

**Decisive discovery test:** Ask a Multi Green/selected-division operations owner to walk through a recent demand/procurement/production/logistics conflict, the actual systems/screens used, decision history, resolution time and whether any part remains materially manual or delayed. A complete, sufficient workflow in existing software or dependence on unsupported manufacturing telemetry disqualifies the proposed pilot lane.

### Masterparts: what is already covered
- Account-holder online ordering is documented: https://www.masterparts.com/online-shop-2/
- Masterparts documented SkyNet customer order tracking and its value for workshop estimates in its August 14, 2018 post: https://www.masterparts.com/blog/auto-parts-online-convenient-new-tracking-system-our-customers/
- Modderfontein is a distribution centre with no sales counter; other Gauteng branches are Hennopspark, Montana and Randburg: https://www.masterparts.com/branches/
- Modderfontein warehouse-role advertisement dated October 2, 2026 lists bulk receiving, picking, packing, freight preparation and stock counts: https://jobs.recruite.co.za/job/stores-person
- Official business entry: Modderfontein DC/call centre +27 10 630 0757; head office Epping +27 21 505 5757. These are general contact routes, not a confirmed operations sponsor: https://www.masterparts.com/contact/

**Audit interpretation:** Do not pitch customer tracking, basic ordering or a replacement warehouse platform. Internal inter-branch exception management, decision ownership and priority handling remain unverified; absence of public evidence cannot be used as proof of a gap.

**Decisive discovery test:** Ask about a recent urgent customer part request that could not be satisfied normally. How did branch/DC staff determine alternatives and competing priority; who approved a change; how was the commitment updated; what confirmed completion? Reject this lane if existing tools already deliver sufficient joined-up coordination, incidents are too infrequent, or the source system is not integrable within the approved pilot.

### Cross-company minimum useful proof and boundary
For any qualified opportunity, require a documented abnormal operational event, an initial authoritative source/data path that SynapseCore supports, relevant company-approved stock/warehouse/fulfilment reference data, clear operator authority and a baseline for detection, assignment, decision, resolution, and manual investigation effort. Hypothetical scenarios remain nonexecuting/advisory. Recommendations and approvals cannot be sold as autonomous source-system actions. Cross-system observability not covered by supported contracts cannot be implied from an order webhook alone. Codex must verify necessary endpoints, mapping, data lifecycle, roles, concurrency and realistic volume before an offer; M6 and M7 gates remain mandatory.
