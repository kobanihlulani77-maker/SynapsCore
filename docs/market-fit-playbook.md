# Market Fit Playbook

This playbook explains where SynapseCore fits in the market, where it is strongest today, and where current maturity still limits the right kind of customer.

## Positioning Rule

SynapseCore should be positioned as a live operations coordination platform for companies that have real operational pressure and fragmented system truth.

It should not be positioned as:

- a general ERP replacement
- a broad connector marketplace
- a simple inventory app
- a pure BI dashboard

## 1. Logistics Companies

### Typical pains

- multiple inbound order or shipment sources
- connector fragility
- weak visibility across dispatch, warehouse, and partner systems
- incident ownership confusion

### Where SynapseCore fits

- connector visibility
- replay and recovery
- runtime trust
- order and fulfillment awareness
- scenario and approval control

### Modules that matter most

- dashboard
- integrations
- replay queue
- orders
- runtime
- approvals/scenarios

### Future opportunities

- stronger transport and lane intelligence
- partner and carrier signal depth
- deeper delay and exception routing

## Who Is Too Small For It

SynapseCore is probably too much for:

- very small companies with one warehouse and simple operations
- businesses that only need light inventory records
- teams with no real integration or operational recovery pressure
- companies looking only for reporting or simple dashboards

## Who Needs Lighter Tooling

Lighter tooling may be better when a company primarily needs:

- simple catalog maintenance
- simple stock counting
- very low-volume order administration
- basic KPI dashboards

SynapseCore is strongest when the operational cost of fragmentation is already painful.

## Current Enterprise-Readiness Limits

The current system is meaningful, but not yet fully enterprise-ready in every dimension.

Honest current limits include:

- narrow connector breadth
- deployment/runtime hardening still in progress
- backend availability still needs stronger proof under degraded dependency conditions
- broader large-scale policy and provisioning posture is still evolving
- scale hardening for very large deployments is not yet fully proven

## Bottom Line

SynapseCore fits best where operational fragmentation already causes cost, delay, ambiguity, and recovery pain.

It is strongest where a company needs one live coordination layer above existing systems, not another isolated tool beside them.
