## 2. Renttech SA — strong new Customer-1 geometry

### Verified public operating model

Renttech SA is a Bidvest company supplying industrial and welding equipment through sales and rentals.

Public company material reports:
- 14 national branches;
- rental and sales operations;
- industrial equipment across welding, power generation, lifting, rigging and construction-related categories;
- service from site establishment through project completion;
- emergency support numbers across multiple branches.

Sources:
- https://renttechsa.co.za/
- https://renttechsa.co.za/about-us/
- https://renttechsa.co.za/services/
- https://renttechsa.co.za/contact-us/

A current September 2026 Rental Administrator role gives unusually useful operating detail. It connects:
- quotations/sales;
- rental documentation;
- K8;
- spreadsheets;
- invoicing;
- customer documents;
- equipment spares;
- machines being serviced/repaired;
- deliveries;
- rental management and branch administration.

Source:
- https://renttechsa.co.za/careers/rental-administrator/

This public evidence does **not** prove Renttech has a fragmented-system problem. It does show a connected operating responsibility spanning customer demand, rental assets, spares, service/repair, documentation and delivery.

### Ownership

Renttech's current management page identifies **Duncan Whitehead — Managing Director**.

Source:
- https://renttechsa.co.za/about-us/

Renttech's public PAIA/POPIA material lists the business email:
- d.whitehead@renttechsa.co.za

Source:
- https://renttechsa.co.za/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/rentech-2021-paia-manual-Website.pdf

### Whole-picture SynapseCore hypothesis

A meaningful operating loop could span:

**customer/project requirement -> quote/commitment -> equipment availability -> rental allocation -> spares/service condition -> branch/service capacity -> delivery/site handoff -> customer project outcome -> return/service/recovery**

The pilot must not become rental analytics or stock monitoring.

The question is whether selected authoritative operational data across the relevant domains can allow SynapseCore to maintain one connected control picture across the chosen branch/rental/service scope.

### Action

Whole-picture outreach sent directly to Duncan Whitehead on 2026-10-07.

The message:
- defines SynapseCore as the whole connected operational intelligence/control system;
- explicitly says it is not replacing K8, ERP, rental, inventory or service systems;
- asks whether the wider operating picture is already controlled coherently;
- preserves the selective-data/whole-control distinction.

### Current judgement

**High-priority first-pilot candidate.**

Why:
- bounded national/branch geometry;
- direct executive route;
- rentals + assets + spares + repair + delivery adds a different operational archetype;
- downstream customer/project consequence is visible;
- less enterprise procurement complexity than Shoprite/Famous Brands/RCL.

### Ownership

AfriSam identifies **Glenn Johnson — Construction Materials Operations Executive** on its current executive management page.

Source:
- https://www.afrisam.co.za/about-us/

A public statutory AfriSam document lists Glenn's business email:
- glenn.johnson@za.afrisam.com

Source:
- https://www.afrisam.co.za/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/SLP-2023-2027-PMB-Quarry-11-March-2025-Sec-29.pdf

### Whole-picture SynapseCore hypothesis

Construction materials creates a different operational timing problem:

**customer/project demand -> product/mix requirement -> production capacity -> material availability -> plant/quarry condition -> batching/dispatch -> fleet/logistics -> delivery window -> site/customer outcome**

AfriSam also publicly notes that project-scope changes can propagate through the supply chain and that production/delivery planning must support high-volume projects.

This is precisely a cross-domain control question, but SynapseCore must not claim quality/batching authority unless explicitly supported.

### Action

Whole-picture outreach sent directly to Glenn Johnson on 2026-10-07.

The message:
- acknowledges ClickToGo and existing planning capability;
- does not assume a technology gap;
- asks about the broader control picture when production, material availability, plant conditions, logistics and customer commitments interact;
- explicitly avoids ERP/order/dispatch/fleet replacement positioning.

### Current judgement

**Strong industrial strategic candidate with potentially good regional pilot geometry.**

A future scope could potentially use a selected Gauteng construction-material network or other bounded operating slice, subject to discovery and technical support.
