# Research Report 15 — Public Operational Signals and Full-Loop Prospect Expansion
**Date:** 2026-10-09 (Africa/Johannesburg)
**Status:** External company research and founder-led outreach intelligence; **not** proof of company defects, internal architecture, opportunity qualification, technical integration feasibility, meeting acceptance, or pilot approval.
**Companion records:** [Product research foundation](synapsecore-product-problem-market-research-foundation-2026-10.md), [Master readiness map](SYNAPSCORE-MASTER-ENGINEERING-READINESS-MAP.md), [Founder messaging standard](founder-outreach-messaging-standard-2026-10-03.md), [Outreach log](pilot-outreach-log-q4-2026.md).

## Founder's operational direction

Expand outreach steadily across serious companies, including comparatively smaller companies and bounded company units, wherever the **entire supported SynapseCore control loop** can operate. Do not equate 'small footprint' with a one-connector, one-domain, logistics-only or dashboard-only pilot. For a pilot, a selected region, business unit, factory-plus-branches or DC-plus-stores is a footprint boundary, *not* a product reduction.

Use both (1) verified company structure, scale, business lines and management statements and (2) public symptom signals such as complaints, posted employee descriptions, service promises, forums and company changes, to produce **testable hypotheses**. We cannot inspect private internal systems before discovery. Social posts and reviews are neither root-cause evidence nor statistically representative failure rates. A large operating network is a plausible source of coordination pressure, not evidence that the company is dysfunctional.

Never let the founder's last example or correction crowd out the rest of the system. **Full product frame:** authoritative source truth and connector freshness -> contextual live picture across multiple operational domains -> conditions/dependencies/risk/pressure -> attention/priority/recommendations -> governed human decision/approval/alert/escalation -> only verified supported actions or accountable external handoff -> observed outcome and authoritative readback -> audit/realtime convergence -> retries/replay/recovery with role/tenant/security boundaries.

**Current technical gate:** Distinguish the end-M product identity from the confirmed M1 supported integration surface. No universal production/finance/field-service/retail system adapters, future AI features, external autonomous writeback, company data connections, or M6/M7 acceptance can be implied by this research.

## Practical discovery method

1. Map a company's normal full business journey and at least one routine period of competing priorities.
2. Map a credible compound scenario joining at least three business domains; include a customer/service outcome, responsible humans and real source-of-truth owners.
3. Find externally observable signals: complaints about missing/late/wrong stock, conflicting delivery status, order cancellations, refund friction, service delays, outages; alternatively company-admitted KPI risks, expansion/operating-model resets and public operational investments.
4. State **publicly verified fact**, **individual unverified allegation**, **our hypothesis**, and **company-confirmed discovery** distinctly. Never email a company saying its software is broken based on anecdotes.
5. Identify operations/value owner and executive route; check Gmail for duplicate/similar prior contacts before sending.
6. First meeting tests present-day coordination, alternatives, authority, data availability, outcome readback, operational metrics, integration rights and economic value. If the company already has comprehensive systems, test **incremental benefit**, not a presumed gap.
7. Pilot candidate only after customer dialogue plus initial technical feasibility, M6 and M7 gates, and commercial/legal agreement.

## Proposed first conversations and qualification

**Conversations not technical selling:** Ask (1) What are the team's shared operational priorities and who owns decisions? (2) Where does the organization already have a trusted unified view? (3) Which inter-domain decisions require negotiation or escalation? (4) How is completion verified after a decision/action? (5) Are source data and identities available securely on a contract we could support without new unverified adapters? (6) What measurable improvement, if any, would justify a controlled study?

**Selection preference:** A medium-sized, vertically integrated operator like Agrico or Dairy Group may yield a more complete verified loop within a manageable approved operating corridor than a giant network where only parcel events are accessible. Conversely, large sites like Boxer/Italtile/Takealot remain strategic if one bounded operating corridor gives enough reliable cross-domain source facts and human governance.
