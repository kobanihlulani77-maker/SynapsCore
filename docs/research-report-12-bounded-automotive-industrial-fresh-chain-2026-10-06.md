### Why the segment fits the SynapseCore research question

The relevant operating problem can span:
**international/local sourcing -> wholesale distribution -> distribution points -> owned/franchised retail channels -> workshop/customer demand -> changing availability/priority -> decision/handoff -> confirmed service outcome**.

This is not a parts-ordering product definition. The point is the cross-channel operating control across a complex value chain.

### Existing maturity matters

Motus explicitly describes:
- vertical integration;
- operational enhancement;
- digitisation/innovation;
- growing integration across wholesale and retail channels;
- direct shipment trials and other supply-chain changes.

Therefore discovery must ask what is already solved before claiming a gap.

The message:
- identifies a South African founder;

- defines SynapseCore broadly;
- explicitly says it is not replacing ERP/warehouse/retail/parts systems;
- asks about cross-functional operational control across sourcing, distribution, channels, priorities, decisions and outcomes.

## 4. Food Lover's Market / FVC International — strong fit, hold outreach

### Verified public structure

Food Lover's Market says the Group includes:
- Food Lover's Market retail;
- FreshStop;
- FVC International;
- Cape Roasters;
- other related brands.

It describes FVC International as:
- supplying stores in the group with fresh produce daily;
- a leading South African fresh-produce importer/exporter;
- exporting to more than 35 countries;
- operating a distribution network across South Africa.

Sources:
- https://foodloversmarket.co.za/about-us/
- https://foodloversmarket.co.za/earth-lovers-post/a-journey-to-industry-leadership/

Food Lover's own supply-chain description says logistics supports:
- transport solutions;
- problem-solving/crisis management;
- strategic decisions;
- distribution centres/stores;
- proactive and reactive action;
- end-to-end service.

Source:
https://foodloversmarket.co.za/earth-lovers-post/default-29/

### Why it strongly fits the product thesis

Fresh operations create different time horizons and state semantics:
- demand changes rapidly;
- shelf life/perishability matters;
- imports/exports introduce external dependencies;
- quality/temperature state can override apparent physical availability;
- stores need reliable supply;
- logistics and supplier conditions can change quickly.

This matches the broader research finding that **physical quantity is not the same as operational availability**.

### Why outreach is held

Food/fresh operations introduce:
- quality authority;
- temperature/cold-chain state;
- food safety;
- spoilage/perishability;
- possibly supplier certification/compliance.

For an early pilot, SynapseCore must not be framed as making or overriding safety/quality decisions.

Before outreach, identify:
1. the senior cross-functional supply-chain/logistics owner;
2. current central systems/control structure;
3. a non-safety-critical pilot boundary;
4. which outcomes can be measured without entering quality authority.

### Current decision

**Research target, not active outreach yet.**

This is sequencing, not a judgement that the company is a weak fit.
