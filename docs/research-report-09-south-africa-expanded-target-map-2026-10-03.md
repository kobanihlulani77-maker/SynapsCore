# SynapseCore Research Report 09 — Expanded South Africa Target Map
**Date:** 2026-10-03  
**Purpose:** Expand the South African opportunity map beyond the original first/second-wave companies while preserving the complete SynapseCore operating thesis.

## 1. Executive conclusion

The expanded search confirms that South Africa contains several additional operating environments where the complete SynapseCore problem can exist:

- industrial distribution + production + SAP + regional operations;
- vertically integrated food manufacturing + logistics + franchise networks;
- automotive import/distribution + aftermarket parts + retail/dealer networks;
- pharmacy/health retail + wholesale + e-commerce + regulated distribution;
- fresh-food production/distribution + retail supply networks.

The strongest new practical target is **Macsteel**.
The strongest new strategic full-engine target is **Famous Brands**.
**Motus Aftermarket Parts** is strategically relevant but larger and more complex.
**Dis-Chem** is highly relevant conceptually but too regulated and enterprise-heavy for a first deployment.
**Food Lover's Market** remains a useful research candidate, especially for fresh-product and supply-chain operations, but needs deeper systems/ownership research before outreach.

## 2. Macsteel — strongest new practical target

### Verified public evidence
Macsteel operates a wide South African branch/business-unit footprint including:
- service centres;
- trading branches;
- tube & pipe;
- coil processing;
- roofing;
- fluid control;
- special steels;
- VRN operations;
- exports;
- e-commerce.

Public branch information shows several Gauteng facilities across Boksburg, Germiston, Elandsfontein, Roodekop and Pretoria.

Source:
https://macsteel.co.za/branch-locator/

A current October 2026 Macsteel Logistics Supervisor role at Mac City/Elandsfontein explicitly connects:
- warehouse operations;
- inventory control;
- SAP stock accuracy;
- dispatch/loading priorities;
- production;
- planning;
- customer requirements;
- customer commitments;
- equipment breakdowns;
- financial performance;
- SHEQ/safety.

Source:
https://macsteel.erecruit.co/candidateapp/Jobs/View/MCS260930-3

### Why this matters
This is not merely a warehouse problem. The role itself shows a connected operational environment where:
**inventory state + production/planning + dispatch capacity + equipment + customer requirements + delivery timing + commercial performance**
must stay coherent.

That is a strong SynapseCore research fit.

### Current decision
**Move into active discovery.**

A product-first outreach was sent on 2026-10-03 to Macsteel's official info@macsteel.co.za business route, asking to be connected to broader operations/supply-chain/cross-functional performance leadership in Gauteng.

The message explicitly states:
- South African founder context;
- complete SynapseCore product identity;
- no intent to replace SAP/WMS/production systems;
- why Macsteel specifically was selected;
- desire to understand the wider operating environment before proposing anything.

### Candidate discovery area
Gauteng / Mac City ecosystem, potentially connecting selected business units, production/planning, warehouse/dispatch and customer commitments.

This is only a hypothesis until company discovery confirms scope and source architecture.

## 3. Famous Brands — strongest new strategic full-engine target

### Verified public operating model
Famous Brands describes a vertically integrated Supply Chain spanning:
- Manufacturing;
- Logistics;
- Retail;
- procurement;
- franchise partner support.

Its current supply-chain footprint includes:
- 10–11 manufacturing plants depending reporting period/scope;
- nine logistics sites / distribution centres in current public material;
- three cross-dock facilities;
- 101 trucks;
- 33 owner-driver businesses plus external logistics partners;
- exports;
- thousands of restaurant/franchise locations;
- retail products supplied into major retailers.

Sources:
- https://famousbrands.co.za/supply-chain/
- https://famousbrands.co.za/
- https://famousbrands.co.za/iar2025/operations/supply-chain-performance.php

### Technology and operating context
Public reporting describes:
- a single WMS rolled out across distribution sites;
- route-planning improvements;
- upgraded franchise ordering;
- manufacturing technology;
- plans to extract more value from manufacturing and logistics data;
- efforts to better align supply-chain production with demand from Brands;
- owner-driver and external logistics relationships;
- franchise partner service expectations;
- supply-chain performance measured against on-time/in-full/error-free delivery.

Sources:
- https://famousbrands.co.za/iar2025/operations/supply-chain-performance.php
- https://famousbrands.co.za/iar2025/strategy/consumer-technology-investments.php
- https://famousbrands.co.za/iar2025/strategy/ceos-report.php

### Why it is strategically important
Famous Brands exposes almost the complete intended SynapseCore operating environment:
**market/franchise demand -> procurement -> manufacturing -> inventory -> logistics -> external delivery partners -> franchise/customer outcome -> operational learning**

It also already has strong specialist systems, meaning any SynapseCore value would need to be truly cross-domain and incremental.

### Current decision
**Strategic research target — do not send generic inbox outreach yet.**

Reason:
- the company is sophisticated;
- it has mature supply-chain investments;
- the opportunity, if any, would likely sit at a senior cross-functional level;
- a weak contact route could misclassify SynapseCore as WMS/logistics software.

Next step:
identify the appropriate Supply Chain / Operations / systems-control leader or a credible warm/direct route before outreach.

- 323 retail dealerships in South Africa;
- 130 vehicle-rental branches/kiosks;
- roughly 470–530 aftermarket-parts outlets/agencies depending current source/reporting date;
- integrated import/distribution activities;
- broad automotive retail and aftermarket operations.

Sources:
- https://www.motus.co.za/about-us/operational-footprint/
- https://www.motus.co.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Pre-close_call_in_respect_of_the_financial_year_ending_30_June_2026.pdf

Current roles show WMS-driven inbound, branch logistics, supplier coordination, inventory accuracy and operational KPIs.

### Why it matters
Motus contains several SynapseCore-relevant operating structures inside one group:
- import/distribution;
- aftermarket parts;
- branch operations;
- supplier flows;
- retail/customer service;
- delivery/logistics;
- digital/financial channels.

### Current decision
**Strategic watch/deep-research target, not immediate outreach.**

Reason:
scale is very high and the group contains many distinct businesses. We first need to identify a bounded business unit where a complete control loop can be investigated without making SynapseCore look like a generic automotive-parts platform.

## 5. Dis-Chem — powerful problem fit, high first-pilot burden

### Verified public operating model
Dis-Chem describes itself as integrated across:
- procurement;
- wholesale distribution;
- retail;
- e-commerce;
- patient/health services.

It had 316 retail pharmacy stores and 42 baby stores at FY2026 year-end, while wholesale revenue served both its own stores and external pharmacies/franchisees.

Sources:
- https://dischemgroup.com/about-us/
- https://dn.sharenet.co.za/v3/sens_display.php?seq=3&tdate=20260529070500

Dis-Chem says it has six state-of-the-art distribution centres with temperature-controlled environments and stock systems designed for pharmaceutical batch expiry, stock rotation and promotion-driven volume spikes.

Source:
https://dischemgroup.com/sustainability/how-we-impact-the-world/

SAP publicly documents Dis-Chem's use of SAP EWM and automation in warehouse operations.

Source:
https://www.sap.com/documents/2025/03/9427bb3b-fb7e-0010-bca6-c68f7e60039b.html

### Why it matters
This is a rich full-system environment with:
- retail;
- wholesale;
- regulated stock;
- distribution;
- e-commerce;
- healthcare;
- external customers;
- complex system authority.

### Current decision
**Market benchmark / later strategic target.**

Reason:
pharmaceutical compliance, safety, scale and mature SAP capability make this an unnecessarily heavy first deployment environment.

Research it to understand enterprise requirements, not because it is the easiest first customer.

## 8. What this expansion tells us about SynapseCore

The research is not converging on one industry.

Instead, the repeated pattern is:

**multiple authoritative systems + multiple operational domains + changing conditions + dependencies + competing priorities + human authority + external partners + need for verified outcomes and recovery**

This appears in:
- industrial materials;
- automotive parts;
- food manufacturing/distribution;
- wholesale;
- retail;
- logistics;
- healthcare distribution.

That is evidence that the product thesis is broader than an order/warehouse workflow.

It does not prove universal demand. Each company still needs customer-specific discovery.
