## 1. Governing rule

Target qualification now follows:

> **Whole-picture control system, selective data footprint.**

A company is not excluded because it already has an ERP, WMS, TMS, control tower, automation, strong internal IT, or large scale.

The pilot does not replicate every enterprise system or all company data. However, the selected data contract must be sufficient to exercise the connected SynapseCore control engine across the relevant supported domains. A connector demonstration or one-domain dashboard is not enough.

## 2. Food Lover's Market — move to active discovery

### Verified operating environment

Food Lover's Market Holdings includes retail operations and affiliated operating businesses including FVC International and Cape Roasters.

FVC International supplies Food Lover's Market group stores with fresh produce daily, imports fresh produce into South Africa, exports internationally and operates a national distribution network.

Food Lover's Market has also publicly described centralised logistics and a dedicated fleet supporting regional DC operations.

Public sources:
- https://foodloversmarket.co.za/about-us/
- https://cdn.foodloversmarket.co.za/wp-content/uploads/20240801161442/Food-Lovers-Holdings-Sustainability-Report-2023.pdf

### Existing control-tower evidence

A public Food Lover's Market recruitment post for a **Control Tower Specialist** stated that the role:
- reports to the National Logistics Manager;
- builds a control-tower platform for the group;
- manages daily logistics and supply-chain operations;
- provides end-to-end visibility of shipments, inventory and supply-chain flows;
- detects disruptions/delays/exceptions;
- coordinates procurement, warehouse, customer service, carriers, suppliers and 3PLs;
- supports incident/crisis management and real-time decisions.

The post was shared by **Edwin Deyzel**, identified publicly as National Logistics Manager.

Source:
- https://za.linkedin.com/in/edwin-deyzel-69639b20b

### Why this makes the account more useful, not less

Food Lover's Market already attempting to build control-tower capability is not a reason to exclude it.

It creates a high-quality discovery question:

> What does the current control tower actually control and understand, where does its scope stop, and is there incremental value in a broader SynapseCore whole-operation intelligence/control layer across the chosen operating scope?

SynapseCore must not be pitched as:
- another shipment dashboard;
- another logistics control tower;
- a replacement for TMS/WMS/ERP;
- a quality/food-safety authority.

The discussion must test whether the complete SynapseCore loop adds anything beyond the existing logistics/control-tower capability.

### Pilot geometry hypothesis

A possible future scope could be a selected regional operating slice containing the relevant connected facts for:
- demand/order state;
- catalog/product identity;
- inventory/reservation state;
- fulfillment/service state;
- integration/freshness;
- operational conditions/pressure;
- alerts/recommendations;
- operator/governance activity;
- outcomes/readback;
- replay/recovery.

Food quality, temperature-release authority and food-safety decisions remain outside SynapseCore unless explicitly supported and approved.

### Action

Product-first whole-picture outreach sent 2026-10-07 directly to **Edwin Deyzel, National Logistics Manager**, using the public business address `edeyzel@fvc.co.za` that appeared in the company's control-tower recruitment material.

The email explicitly:
- recognizes existing control-tower capability;
- does not assume a gap;
- explains the whole-operation SynapseCore model;
- distinguishes selective data volume from reduced product scope;
- asks how far the current control picture reaches and whether broader value exists.

### Strong direct control-loop evidence

A June 2026 Multi Green Operations Coordinator role says the operation coordinates:
- orders;
- production planning;
- inventory;
- logistics;
- stakeholders;
- service delivery;
- profitability;
- operational optimisation.

Source:
- https://www.myjobmag.co.za/job/operations-coordinator-multi-green-villiers-vkb-group

This is direct evidence of the connected operating structure SynapseCore is meant to understand.

### Correct pilot interpretation

Multi Green does not require SynapseCore to clone a production ERP/MRP system.

But neither should the pilot be reduced to order analytics.

The correct question is whether the relevant authoritative facts across the chosen operating scope can enter SynapseCore so it can build the connected control picture across order/demand state, inventory, fulfillment/operational progression, integration state, intelligence, operator decisions, outcome and recovery.

If essential production facts are required to make that picture truthful, they must be supported/approved rather than ignored. If the selected scope can be truthfully controlled using currently supported domains, full MRP integration is unnecessary.

The email asks for a 20–30 minute operational discussion and explicitly invites Jannie Willemse or the appropriate senior owner into the conversation if needed.

## 4. Strategic significance

These two companies test two different realities:

### Food Lover's Market
A sophisticated organisation already building a control-tower function. The question is **incremental whole-operation control value** beyond existing logistics capability.

### Multi Green
A production/distribution company where current public role design already exposes orders, production, inventory and logistics as one connected operating responsibility.

Both are valuable precisely because SynapseCore is not defined by whether the customer has weak or strong technology.
