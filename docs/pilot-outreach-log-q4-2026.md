### Outreach discipline
- Discovery first; no product demo forced into the first contact.
- No claim that current ERP/WMS/CRM/order systems are deficient.
- No invented ROI, savings or problem severity.
- No promise of autonomous procurement, stock transfer, delivery or safety-critical execution.
- If a response arrives, replace public hypotheses with company-confirmed facts immediately.
- Technical feasibility enters only after the operating-value hypothesis survives discovery.
- M6/M7 remain the technical launch gates; commercial outreach does not bypass them.
